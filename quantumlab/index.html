QuantumLab

QuantumLab explains the ideas in our lab project, which reproduces the GLOBECOM 2024 paper on multipath entanglement distribution by Halder, Matus and Fettweis. It is a teaching companion by Panhapiseth Lim and Victor Akinmade at UTPB. It is not graded work.

How to use it

  • Each page asks you to do one thing: flip coins, drag a slider, press a button, or click cities.
  • Before each run you make a guess. The page then tells you whether you were right.
  • Every idea has a kid line and a student line. The "?" button shows the expert line and where the simple picture stops being true.
  • Stuck? Press the help button, or the H key, to climb five levels of help: nudge, hint, stronger hint, show me, do it for me. Asking for help costs nothing.
  • Drag the 3D picture to turn it, scroll to zoom. Your progress is saved only in this browser.
